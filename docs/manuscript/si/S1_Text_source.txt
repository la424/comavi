## Section 1. FoldX calculations

%from: scripts/comavi_v7/foldx_runner.py
Each structure was repaired once with RepairPDB, and the repaired file was cached and reused for every variant scored on it. Variants were written in FoldX notation (wild-type residue, chain, residue number, substituted residue). BuildModel was run with five replicates on the isolated-subunit model and on the assembled complex. AnalyseComplex was run on the repaired wild-type complex and on each of the five mutant complexes, once for each pairing of the variant's chain with a partner chain. No FoldX option other than those shown was set, so every energy-function parameter is the FoldX 5.1 default [@schymkowitz2005; @delgado2019].

@code foldx --command=RepairPDB --pdb=<structure> --output-dir=<workdir>
@code foldx --command=BuildModel --pdb=<repaired structure> --mutant-file=individual_list.txt --numberOfRuns=5 --output-dir=<workdir>
@code foldx --command=AnalyseComplex --pdb=<complex> --analyseComplexChains=<variant chain>,<partner chain> --output-dir=<workdir>

%from: scripts/comavi_v7/foldx_runner.py
Folding ΔΔG is the mutant minus wild-type BuildModel energy averaged over the five replicates, so positive values are destabilizing. The wild-type interaction energy with a partner was calculated once, on the repaired complex, and the mutant interaction energy on each replicate mutant complex; binding ΔΔG for that partner is the mean over replicates of mutant minus wild-type interaction energy. The replicate standard deviation is kept for every axis and partner.

## Section 2. Structural-context tier: weights and cut-offs

%from: scripts/comavi_v7/mechanism.py compute_disruption_score; scripts/comavi_v7/constants.py; scripts/comavi_v7/structure_loading.py; scripts/verify_tier_construction.py
The tier is calculated from the structure alone and contains no energy. Substitution severity is the Grantham distance [@grantham1974] divided by 53.75 and capped at 4. The contact count of the variant residue is the number of residues of its own chain with any atom within 5 Å of any of its atoms, excluding the two sequence neighbours on either side, plus the largest number of residues within 5 Å in any one partner chain that passes the confidence gate. A partner passes the gate when the model confidence (pLDDT) at the variant site in that complex is at least 50; partners in experimental structures always pass. Burial uses the relative solvent accessibility of the residue, its Shrake–Rupley accessible surface area [@shrake1973] calculated with Biopython [@cock2009] divided by the theoretical maximum for its residue type [@tien2013], and takes the most buried state across the isolated subunit and the complexes with confidence-passing partners. The best pLDDT at the site is the higher of its isolated-subunit and complex values. Table A gives the points and cut-offs.

@tablecap Table A. Components and cut-offs of the structural-context tier.
@tabhead Component | Definition | Points
@tabrow Context | Severity × contact count: 20 or more; 10 to <20; 4 to <10; 1 to <4; below 1 | 4; 3; 2; 1; 0
@tabrow Interface | Contact with one confidence-passing partner; with two or more | 1.5; 2.0
@tabrow Burial | Partially buried (relative accessibility 0.10 to <0.25); buried core (below 0.10) | 1.0; 2.0
@tabrow Confidence | Best site pLDDT 50 to <70; below 50; experimental structure | ×0.7; ×0.4; ×1

## Section 3. Statistical conventions, seeds and draw counts

%from: scripts/build_evidence_sensitivity.py; verification/stress_tests.py; scripts/verify_tier_construction.py
Proportions carry exact binomial 95% confidence intervals, and Fisher exact tests are two-sided. The within-system permutation tests of the tier screens are exact: each enumerates the convolution of the per-system hypergeometric distributions, so no seed is involved. Resampling intervals are percentile intervals. P(margin ≤ 0) is the fraction of resamples in which COMAVI's margin over the strongest structure-blind strategy is zero or negative, with that strategy re-selected among all 31 labels inside every resample. Values were computed at full precision and rounded once, half to even. Table B lists every resampling analysis with its unit, draw count and seed; scripts are in scripts/ except stress_tests.py, which is in verification/.

## Section 4. Evidence provenance and evidence-restricted analyses

%from: reference_outputs/COMAVI_evidence_ledger.csv
The 56 benchmark variants carry 97 committed energetic-axis expectations (S1 Table): 30 on isolated-subunit stability, 25 on assembled-complex stability and 42 on binding, on 52 variants; the other four are graded only at the whole-variant level. By evidence class, 29 are E1 quantitative energetic, 11 E2 quantitative functional, 30 E3 qualitative experimental, 9 E4 population frequency and 18 E5 inferred. The ledger also holds the two expectations of BRCA1 R1699L and BRCA1 R1699Q, which were scored but excluded from the benchmark (S2 Table, excluded_variants sheet). E4 and E5 commitments are heavily enriched for neutral expectations: all 9 E4 axes and all 18 E5 axes are neutral. Assembled-complex stability is the least independently grounded axis: 14 of its 25 committed axes rest on inferred rather than direct or coupled evidence, and 11 are E5 inferred axis states.

%from: reference_outputs/COMAVI_evidence_stratified_agreement.json
Of the 97 expectations, 79 can be compared with a prediction: on every energy axis they clear the model-confidence gate and the check that the interval of the predicted energy, the replicate mean ± 1.96 replicate standard deviations, excludes zero. The other 18 are removed by the interval check; the confidence gate removes none, and none is removed by missing curation. On these 79 axes, direction agreement was 22/34 = 0.647 for E1–E2 evidence and 37/45 = 0.822 for E3–E5 evidence (Fisher p = 0.116). Evidence class was strongly associated with the expected axis state: direct measurements were preferentially available for destabilizers, whereas frequency-supported and inferred commitments were predominantly neutral. Within expected-destabilizing axes, agreement was 16/26 for E1–E2 and 5/7 for E3–E5; within expected-neutral axes, the corresponding rates were 6/7 and 32/38. The Mantel–Haenszel odds ratio across expected-state strata was 0.79 (continuity-corrected test p = 0.98), so evidence class does not explain direction agreement once the expected axis state is considered. Among 25 gradeable E1 energetic axes, 18 agreed (0.720).

%from: reference_outputs/COMAVI_tier_construction.json evidence_restricted_views
For the tier screen, requiring every committed axis to have E1–E3 support leaves 26 of the 46 prioritization variants, 16 structural and 10 non-disruptive (tier sensitivity 0.812, 95% CI 0.544–0.960; specificity 0.600, 0.262–0.878). Requiring every committed axis to be direct or coupled leaves the same 26 variants and the same values. Recovery here rests on 16 positives, of which 13 are recovered, so the interval is wide; on the whole population the screen recovers 17 of 21 (sensitivity 0.810).

%from: reference_outputs/COMAVI_evidence_sensitivity.json views
Table C reports the mechanism-pattern score on evidence-restricted subsets of the benchmark. Removing variants changes the balance of classes, and a structure-blind strategy scores, by construction, the fraction of a subset that it matches, so each subset is compared with its own strongest strategy, re-selected among the 31 labels on that subset. The balanced score, the mean of the mechanism and non-disruptive arms, does not depend on that balance: no structure-blind strategy exceeds 0.5 on it, because a label that accepts every non-disruptive variant recovers no mechanism, and a label that asserts disruption rejects no non-disruptive variant. On the 24 variants whose expectations are all quantitative, only four of them non-disruptive, calling every variant destabilized on all three axes outscores COMAVI, while COMAVI's balanced score stays above 0.5.

## Section 5. Contribution of each system to the margin

%from: reference_outputs/COMAVI_evidence_sensitivity.json by_system
On the full benchmark the strongest structure-blind strategy is to call no variant disrupted, which scores 27.0 of 56 by accepting every non-disruptive variant. COMAVI scores 37.0, a margin of 10.0 grade points. Table D divides that margin among the systems: a system's contribution is the sum, over its variants, of COMAVI's grade minus the grade of calling nothing. The BRCT domain contributes 5.0 and the hemoglobin tetramer 3.5. Three systems reduce the margin, MSH2–MSH6 and troponin I–troponin C by 2.0 each and PI3K by 1.0, because COMAVI predicts disruption for five non-disruptive variants there (MSH2 A636P, MSH2 C697F, PIK3R1 N564D, TNNI3 R145G and TNNI3 R145Q) and earns no credit on the two PI3K mechanisms; three systems contribute nothing. Leaving one system out, against the strongest strategy on the remaining variants, the margin ranges from 0.109 without the BRCT domain to 0.217 without troponin I–troponin C.
