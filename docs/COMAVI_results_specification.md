# COMAVI — Results Specification

**What each result is, what it means, and how it should be interpreted.**

Canonical basis: `reference_outputs/scored_61var_canonical.csv` at the canonical
operating point t = 2.5 kcal/mol, post-v7.4. Every number below is regenerated by
`verification/verify_stage6.py` (41/41), `verification/stress_tests.py`, and the five
scripts in `figures/src/`. Nothing here is transcribed from prose.

---

## 0. The one-sentence claim, and the two it is not

**The claim.** On a 61-variant benchmark spanning 14 systems, COMAVI recovers the
literature-defined *mechanism* of structural disruption at 0.72 mechanism-consistency
and 0.76 per-axis directional agreement, and its axes track directly measured free
energies (fold ρ = 0.72, binding ρ = 0.90) — so the mechanism call is a physical
statement, not a label-fitting exercise.

**Not a pathogenicity claim.** COMAVI emits no pathogenicity verdict, and the paper
must never imply one. The tier gradient (§3) is evidence that mechanism and
pathogenicity are *correlated but distinct*, which is the opposite of a classifier
claim.

**Not an accuracy-race claim against AlphaMissense.** AlphaMissense out-AUCs the
structural tier on this benchmark (0.90 vs 0.77, §7). Saying so plainly is what makes
the orthogonality argument credible. The claim is resolution, not accuracy.

## 1. Composition — what the benchmark actually contains

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| Variants | 61 |
| PPI variants / complexes | 49 / 13 |
| BRCA1-BRCT monomer-fold cohort | 12 |
| Systems | 14 |
| Graded for mechanism-consistency | 57 |
| Gradeable axes (agreement denominator) | 131 |

**Per-axis counts — three definitions, and you must always name which.** This is the
single most reliable source of internal inconsistency in this project; it has been
caught twice.

| | definition | monomer-fold | complex-fold | binding/PPI |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **Annotated destabilizers** — curated expectation on that axis is `destab` | 10 | 11 | **15** |
| **B** | **Gradeable axes** — axis passes the evaluability gate, destabilizing or not | 27 | 25 | **32** |
| **C** | **Graded disruptors** — A ∩ B | 10 | 10 | **13** |

Use **A** for composition ("the axis is supported by n annotated disruptors").
Use **B** for agreement denominators (Fig. 3a). **C** is diagnostic only.
The A→C gaps are three named variants that are annotated but fail the evaluability
gate: PI3K E542K and SMAD4/SMAD3 D351H (binding), MLH1/PMS2 R755W (complex-fold).

**Interpretation.** 61 variants across 14 systems is a *mechanism* benchmark, not a
population benchmark. It is large enough to estimate an agreement rate with a ±0.07
interval and to detect a monotonic tier gradient; it is far too small to make claims
about prevalence, gene-level burden, or clinical performance. Every interval in this
paper should be read as "this is what 61 curated variants support," not "this is what
COMAVI does in the clinic."

## 2. The two headline metrics

| Metric | Value at t = 2.5 | Denominator |
|---|---|---|
| Mechanism-consistency | **0.7193** | 57 graded variants |
| Structural agreement | **0.7557** | 99 / 131 axes |

**Threshold sweep** (the reason to trust the operating point):

| t | 1.0 | 1.5 | 2.0 | **2.5** | Sap-CI |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mechanism-consistency | 0.579 | 0.649 | 0.684 | **0.719** | 0.711 |
| Structural agreement | 0.718 | 0.756 | 0.756 | **0.756** | 0.740 |

**What they mean.** Mechanism-consistency asks a per-variant question: does the
three-axis call reproduce the literature-defined mechanism (consistent 1.0 / partial
0.5 / inconsistent 0.0, averaged)? Structural agreement asks a per-axis question: is
the directional call on this axis correct? They are deliberately different — a variant
can get two of three axes right and score 0.5 on mechanism while contributing 2/3 to
agreement.

**How to interpret.** The load-bearing observation is not the value, it is the
*plateau*. Structural agreement is flat at 0.756 from t = 1.5 to 2.5 — three
consecutive thresholds, identical numerator. That is the signature of an operating
point chosen on a stable region rather than tuned to a peak. Report it that way; a
reviewer's first suspicion of any threshold-parameterized method is tuning, and the
sweep is the pre-emptive answer.

Mechanism-consistency does rise monotonically to 2.5, which is a genuine asymmetry
worth stating rather than hiding: stricter calling suppresses benign over-calls
(helping the structurally-silent class) faster than it abstains true destabilizers.
§4 shows the two classes moving in opposite directions across the sweep, which is the
mechanistic explanation and belongs next to the sweep table.

## 3. The structural-evidence tier gradient

Binning the 49 labeled PPI variants by structural-evidence tier — **which contains no
ΔΔG term** — gives a monotonic pathogenicity gradient:

| Tier | Pathogenic / total | Fraction |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 14 / 14 | 100% |
| 2 | 13 / 18 | 72% |
| 3 | 7 / 10 | 70% |
| 4 | 3 / 7 | 43% |

Spearman ρ = **−0.400**, p = **0.0044** (primary statistic).
Strong (1–2) vs weak (3–4): OR = 3.78, Fisher p = 0.080.

**What it means.** The tier is built from Grantham severity and interface contact
count — geometry and chemistry, no energetics. That it orders pathogenicity at all is
an independent line of evidence that the structural framing is capturing something
real, obtained without the FoldX axes participating.

**How to interpret — and the trap.** Quote the **rank correlation**, not the Fisher
test. On the expanded 61-set the binary strong-vs-weak dichotomy loses significance
(p = 0.080) while the rank correlation strengthens in interpretation. This is not
cherry-picking: a four-level ordinal predictor tested by collapsing to a dichotomy
throws away the ordering that *is* the claim, and the earlier 44-set framing
(OR = 6.48) should be treated as superseded, not as a better result. The honest
sentence is "monotonic ordinal gradient, ρ = −0.40, p = 0.0044; the binary contrast is
underpowered at this n."

Tiers 2 and 3 are statistically indistinguishable (72% vs 70%). Do not narrate a
four-step staircase. The defensible reading is a strong top, an indistinct middle, and
a clearly depleted bottom.

## 4. Mechanism-class competency

Mechanism-consistency by class across the sweep (n sums to the headline 57):

| Class | n | t=1.0 | 1.5 | 2.0 | **2.5** | Sap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| No structural effect | 32 | 0.438 | 0.594 | 0.719 | **0.813** | 0.844 |
| Complex-fold + binding destab. | 9 | 0.889 | 0.889 | 0.667 | **0.667** | 0.611 |
| Binding destab., fold intact | 6 | 0.667 | 0.667 | 0.667 | **0.583** | 0.583 |
| Fold destab. (monomer + complex) | 10 | 0.700 | 0.600 | 0.600 | **0.550** | 0.450 |

**What it means.** The classes move in opposite directions across the threshold. The
silent class improves monotonically (0.44 → 0.81) as stricter calling suppresses
over-calls; every disruptive class degrades as true but modest destabilizers fall below
threshold and get abstained.

**How to interpret.** This is the honest cost statement of the operating point, and it
should be presented as a trade-off rather than buried. At t = 2.5 the pipeline is
tuned toward specificity: it is very good at declining to call a variant disrupted
(0.81) and mediocre at resolving which of several mechanisms a genuine disruptor uses
(0.55–0.67). For a triage application — "which of these 134 CHD variants deserves a
structural look" — that is the right bias. For a mechanism-assignment application on
variants already known to be disruptive, a lower threshold is the better operating
point, and the sweep is published so a user can pick one.

The fold class at 0.55 is the weakest cell in the table. Say so. It is 10 variants and
its interval is wide.

## 5. Physical validation — the axes vs. measured free energy

This is the only place in the paper where the axes are checked against a *measurement*
rather than a human label, and it is the strongest result in the manuscript.

| Anchor | System | Statistic |
|---|---|---|
| Fold axis | BRCA1 tandem BRCT (1JNX), GdmCl ΔG_U–F | ρ = **0.721**, p = 0.019, n = 10 |
| Binding axis | Hemoglobin α1β2 W37 dose-series + N102T | ρ = **0.900**, p = 0.037, n = 5 |

Cross-system corroboration (Supplementary Note S1, SKEMPI 2.0, not pooled into any
headline): barnase–barstar ρ = 0.56 (ex-Glu73), TEM1–BLIP ρ = 0.50, combined ρ = 0.55,
sign-correct on 30/33 destabilizers.

**What it means.** Every other metric in this paper measures agreement with a *label* —
a curated mechanism call or a clinical assignment. Labels can be wrong, and they can be
circular if the curator saw the structure. These two correlations are against numbers
from a calorimeter and a spectrophotometer. They cannot be circular.

**How to interpret.** Lead the Results with this framing and keep the n's visible. ρ =
0.90 on n = 5 is a strong correlation on a small, clean within-system titration; it is
not a general claim about binding-axis accuracy. That general claim is what S1 provides
at the honest, lower value of ρ ≈ 0.5 cross-system — which is what FoldX is documented
to achieve, and which is the number to quote if a reviewer asks "how good is the
binding axis, really." Presenting 0.90 without S1 would be overclaiming; presenting
them together is the argument.

The one documented failure mode belongs in the same breath: buried charged residues in
a charge cluster (barnase Glu73) can invert the predicted sign. This is FoldX's
limitation, it is known, and naming it costs nothing while buying substantial
credibility.

## 6. Robustness

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Permutation null (MC) | observed 0.7193 vs null 0.479 ± 0.050, **p = 0.0005** |
| Permutation null (SA) | observed 0.7557 vs null 0.532 ± 0.045, **p = 0.0005** |
| Leave-one-system-out (MC) | 0.704 – 0.750 |
| Leave-one-system-out (SA) | 0.742 – 0.772 |
| Replicate noise (MC) | 0.721 ± 0.013 |
| Replicate noise (SA) | 0.754 ± 0.004 |
| Variant bootstrap 95% CI (MC) | [0.614, 0.819] |
| Variant bootstrap 95% CI (SA) | [0.682, 0.828] |
| **System cluster bootstrap 95% CI (MC)** | **[0.625, 0.821]** |
| **System cluster bootstrap 95% CI (SA)** | **[0.706, 0.820]** |

**What each buys.** The permutation null answers "could this arise by chance" (no).
Leave-one-system-out answers "is one system carrying the result" (no — the full range
is narrower than the confidence interval). Replicate noise answers "is this an artifact
of FoldX stochasticity" (no — SA moves ±0.004 across replicate draws). The bootstraps
give the sampling interval.

**How to interpret the two bootstraps — this is the subtle one.** Variant-level
bootstrapping treats axes within a system as independent, but they share a structure, a
partner set, and a curator. A reviewer can fairly object that the interval is too
narrow. The cluster bootstrap resamples whole systems, which is the honest analogue,
and it *barely widens*: SA goes [0.682, 0.828] → [0.706, 0.820]. That the two agree is
the actual finding — it means the result is not an artifact of within-system
correlation. Present the cluster interval as primary and the agreement as the point.

**Trap.** With 14 clusters the cluster interval moves ~0.002 across seeds. It is seeded
(`SEED_CLUSTER = 8`) and must be quoted from `verification_output/comavi_stress_tests.csv`,
never transcribed from a previous draft.

## 7. Relationship to AlphaMissense

On the 47 PPI variants carrying an AlphaMissense score (36 pathogenic / 11 benign):

| Classifier | AUC |
|---|---|
| AlphaMissense score | **0.903** |
| Structural-evidence tier | 0.769 |

**What it means.** AlphaMissense is the better pathogenicity classifier on this
benchmark. This is not a concession to be minimized — it is the premise of the
argument. The two tools answer different questions: AlphaMissense returns a scalar
*probability that a variant is bad*; COMAVI returns *which biophysical axis it breaks
and by how much*. A ranking cannot be converted into a mechanism.

**How to interpret.** State the AUC comparison explicitly and early in §3.7, then make
the resolution argument. The argument survives the AUC gap because the two outputs are
not substitutes: for the 20 pathogenic variants in this benchmark that are structurally
silent on all three axes, AlphaMissense correctly flags them and COMAVI correctly
reports no structural mechanism — both are right, and the pair is more informative than
either. Conversely, on the fold-neutral interface disruptors (VWF R1334Q, CFH R78G) the
structural axes localize the defect to the interface, which no scalar score can do.

**Scope caveat that must stay.** This benchmark contains few variants in the regime
where the two tools would most sharply diverge. Do not claim COMAVI catches variants
AlphaMissense misses as a general property; claim that it resolves mechanism where
AlphaMissense returns only magnitude, and point to the two named fold-neutral interface
cases as the demonstration.

## 8. Structural silence is not benignity

36 of 61 variants are silent on all three structural axes. **20 of those 36 are
pathogenic.**

**What it means.** A negative COMAVI result is not evidence of benignity. Roughly half
of the structurally-silent variants in a curated pathogenic-enriched benchmark are
disease-causing through mechanisms the pipeline cannot see: disorder, expression,
splicing, allostery beyond the modeled interface, and interactions with partners not in
the structure.

**How to interpret — this is the paper's applicability domain and the most important
limitation to state clearly.** COMAVI has an asymmetric evidentiary value: a positive
structural call is informative about mechanism; a negative call is uninformative about
pathogenicity. Every downstream use should be framed as *enrichment*, never as
*exclusion*. This sentence belongs in the Abstract, the Discussion, and the README —
it is the single claim most likely to be misused by a reader who skims.

## 9. What to say if a reviewer pushes

| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "You tuned the threshold." | Sweep is published; SA is flat 0.756 across t = 1.5–2.5. Operating point sits on a plateau. |
| "n = 61 is small." | Agreed, and stated. Permutation p = 0.0005 and cluster CI [0.706, 0.820] are what 61 supports. No prevalence or clinical claim is made. |
| "Your intervals assume independence." | They do not — cluster bootstrap resamples whole systems; it barely widens the interval. |
| "AlphaMissense beats you." | On pathogenicity AUC, yes: 0.903 vs 0.769, stated in §3.7. Different question. |
| "Ground truth is curated by you." | Which is why §3.5 exists — two axes are anchored to calorimetric/spectroscopic measurement, and S1 corroborates cross-system on SKEMPI. |
| "One system is carrying it." | Leave-one-system-out range 0.742–0.772, narrower than the CI. |
| "FoldX is unreliable." | Its documented failure mode (buried charge clusters) is reproduced and named in S1 rather than hidden. |
